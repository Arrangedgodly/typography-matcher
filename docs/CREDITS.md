# Credits

## Typefaces

All typefaces are served at runtime via the [Google Fonts CSS API](https://developers.google.com/fonts/docs/css2)
under their original open licenses (SIL Open Font License 1.1 or Apache 2.0 — per-family
terms in the [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts) repository). Their designers
and foundries retain all rights; this project ships no font files, only pairing
metadata (family names, categories, weights, styles).

## Pairings

The pairings in `src/data/pairings.json` are this project's own editorial selections,
composed independently from Google Fonts catalog facts (categories, available
weights/styles, popularity) and ordinary typographic pairing practice —
category contrast, complementary proportions, clear heading/body roles.
Individual combinations that also appear in community pairing galleries
(fontpair.co, Typewolf, fontpairings.net, and others) reflect common
recommendations in the design community, not list-level derivation:
no gallery's list, ordering, or ranking was copied or converted, and no
gallery was scraped. See `docs/ultron/research/R1-pairing-provenance.md`
for the provenance and licensing disposition behind this dataset, and
`docs/ultron/research/T14-curation-notes.md` for the per-pairing curation
rationale.

This notice is courtesy and transparency, not an admission of any legal
obligation over the pairing data.
